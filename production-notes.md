Identifying the correct language for the very many `xml:lang` attributes used for italicised words or phrases has been a challenge, despite considerable research. In some instances the only reference for an italicised word appears to be Kipling's work itself. In such cases, words have been tagged as `xml:lang="ur"` for Urdu since this is the language the protagonist mostly uses. Future producers should be aware that some tags are doubtful, or might be contested.
